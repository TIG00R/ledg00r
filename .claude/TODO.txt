[x] in expenses when i changed the source account ... the calculations are so much off, the numbers aren't changing / updating
    Three separate faults, all of them real:
    - An amount stated in a currency the account does not hold was taken out of the account as
      though it were the account's own. 5,000 pounds spent off a dollar account took five
      thousand dollars — or was refused as being four thousand dollars short. `inAccountQty`
      converts through pounds, the way `asset.add` always has, and the record keeps the amount
      and the currency actually stated. Expenses, giving and income all shared the fault.
    - A correction that moved no money wrote a movement anyway: saving a row with the same
      account, amount, currency and date reversed the movement and posted an identical one, so
      the log gained a correction handing the money back and a second expense taking it out
      again. Net zero, but it reads as money returned by an edit to a note — which is exactly
      what was reported. Where none of the four things a movement is made of has changed, the
      record is corrected in place and nothing moves. Giving keeps the same rule.
    - The timeline did not move because today's statement was frozen by the first tick after
      midnight and left alone until tomorrow, so everything recorded for the rest of the day
      was invisible on it. Today is not history yet: its figure follows what is recorded, every
      day before it stays frozen, and a figure somebody wrote themselves is never touched.
    - Balances themselves were never wrong. A test now walks the whole sequence — record, move
      to another account, correct with the same values, correct the amount, remove — and
      checks the balance after every step.

[x] for any money movement record in the application ... when delete show 2 options: just delete the record, or delete the record and reverse the transaction
    - The bin asks which. "Remove and put the money back" is what it always did — the movement
      is reversed, the balance returns, the log keeps both halves. "Just remove the record"
      takes the row off and leaves what it moved exactly where it is, which is the right answer
      when the money really did move and it is the record that is wrong: the same expense
      entered twice, a gift recorded by two hands.
    - `expense.remove`, `giving.remove` and `metal.removeLot` take `reverse`, true unless said
      otherwise, so nothing that already calls them changes behaviour. Offered on spending,
      giving, the zakat payments and the metal lots — the logs that keep a record of their own
      beside the movement. The movement log itself still only undoes: a movement is not a
      record standing beside something else, it is the something else, and this ledger does not
      erase one.
