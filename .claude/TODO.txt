[x] make an option for selling an asset with a price:
    - `asset.sell` takes the price, its currency, the account the money reached and the day.
      The thing leaves at what it still stands at, the money arrives at what it fetched, and
      the difference is the profit. It is archived — it is not owned any more — and the log
      keeps the movement.
    - What the price is measured against is one reading, `purchaseBasisEgp`, used by the
      sale and by `assets.list` so the card and the receipt cannot disagree:
        · bought outright: the price it was bought at, frozen at `bought_for` the day it was
          added, converted the way the asset itself is. Repricing a flat rewrites what it is
          worth and no longer rewrites what was paid for it.
        · on a plan: the total actually paid so far — the same figure the portfolio shows and
          zakat counts, read the same way `assets.list` reads it.
    - A "Sell" button sits beside the pencil on each card and opens the form on the card. A
      "Sold" panel underneath lists what has been sold with what went in, what it fetched,
      and the profit or loss, with the percentage under it.
    - Migration 41 adds the columns and backfills `bought_for` from what each outright asset
      opened at, which is exactly what was typed when it was added.
