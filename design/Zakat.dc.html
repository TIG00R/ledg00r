Before you rely on this

This is a calculator, not a ruling. The treatments above follow common positions, but scholars differ on several of them — how shares are valued, whether debts are deducted, whether nisab follows gold or silver. Check the choices with someone qualified.
Nisab uses the gold measure — 85 g. The silver measure of 595 g is usually lower, so it brings more people into zakat, and many scholars prefer it. The app cannot offer it yet: the iSagha feed it reads deliberately skips silver purities, so there is no silver price to convert.
The hawl is lunar, 354 days. Your zakat date is a setting, not a guess from the data — the app cannot know when your wealth first passed nisab.
