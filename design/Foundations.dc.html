Top swatch is the value used on the dark theme, bottom the same hue dropped in lightness for the light theme. The hue never changes, so a colour means the same thing in both. Cash is a darker green than the accent on purpose — the accent means "act on this", cash means "this is money you hold", and they should not read as the same signal.

Where a freely-licensed image of the currency exists it is used directly, embedded in the page. Where none does — GBP here — the mark falls back to a drawn note tinted to the account's colour, which is also what any currency added later will get. Sources: US dollar and generic sterling public domain; Egyptian pound CC BY-SA 4.0; UAE dirham and Saudi riyal CC0; the euro note is the ECB's own specimen image, reproduced well under the 75% size limit its terms set. These marks are account identity, not signal — they never mean positive or negative.
