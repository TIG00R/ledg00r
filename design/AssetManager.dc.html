A deduction is an ordinary movement — cash out of the account, equity into the asset, debt down by the same amount — and appears in the money log marked automatic. Nothing is paid silently: if the account is short on the day, the payment is held and flagged rather than forced into an overdraft.

Add an asset

Anything you own gets a node. Whether it came with a debt is a separate question from what it is.

Paid in full
One asset node, no debt. Record where the money came from and it appears in the log as a movement out of that account.
Under an installment plan
Two nodes — what you own and what you owe. The next step builds the plan; every payment moves value from cash into equity and takes the same amount off the debt.
